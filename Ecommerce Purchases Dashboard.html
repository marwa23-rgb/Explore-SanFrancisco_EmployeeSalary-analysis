    About this data

    • 10,000 records, no missing values and no duplicate rows. Purchase prices range from $0.00 to $99.99.
    • Card numbers, security codes, addresses, IP addresses and email names are left out of this page on purpose. Only price, time, language, card provider, browser, email domain, card expiry year and job are used.
    • Averages for small groups swing widely, so treat differences between providers, languages or jobs with few purchases as noise unless they are large.
